Got Questions?

Important Project Guidelines

Read through our terms, details on delivery, minimum order value, and customizable options.

How do starting prices work?

All prices listed on our website are starting rates. The final quote depends on the complexity of design customization, material choices (e.g., acrylic vs foam board, linen paper vs standard stock), rush delivery requests, and printing volumes.

Is there a minimum order value?

Yes, a minimum project order value of ₹500 applies for custom design requests to ensure we can dedicate the proper design care to your event materials.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Digital design draft mockups are ready in 2-3 business days. Print production and deliveries generally take 5-7 business days. Rush orders (delivery under 3 days) may incur additional express service fees.

Do you handle shipping and setups?

For display options (wooden easels and board rentals), we offer display setup & arrangements at the venue starting at ₹999. Shipping costs for bulk return gift bags or printed stationery are calculated at cost.

Can I order design-only files?

Yes! We offer "Design Only" options for all stationery, stickers, tags, and accessories. You will receive high-resolution, print-ready PDF/Vector artwork to print at your local printing press if preferred.

Do you offer package discounts?

Absolutely. Our pre-packaged bundles (Essential, Celebration, Premium) are pre-discounted to offer 15-25% savings compared to selecting items individually. We also offer special bulk pricing for orders of 100+ return gift bags.
